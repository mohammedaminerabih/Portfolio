Analysis Results

Key findings from the Bordeaux Real Estate Analysis — 3,923 cleaned transactions from the 2024 DVF open dataset.

3,923 Clean Transactions
~4,300 Median €/m²
0.15 Price–Surface Correlation
506–12,453 €/m² Range

Price Distribution

Right-skewed distribution centered around 3,000–5,500 €/m². The long tail reflects luxury properties in central Bordeaux.

Houses vs Apartments

Houses show a slightly higher median price per m² than apartments, with significant overlap between both distributions.

Top Postal Codes

The 33000 area (historic center) commands the highest prices. Location dominates pricing more than property size.

Correlation Matrix

Weak linear correlation (r ≈ 0.15) between total price and surface area — confirming that unit price depends heavily on location and quality.
